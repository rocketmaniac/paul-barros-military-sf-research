THE NARRATIVE — Paul Napoleon Barros in Vietnam (July 1963 – January 1964)

Evidence Assessment

DJIRAI (A-33) — CONFIRMED (10 independent lines of evidence)

  1. Family record: Paul's son recorded "assigned to Djirai" in a 2004 note to his brother and recalls his father saying the name — but this is a ~40-year-old memory [2004]. The Central Highlands location is correct.
  2. The Clements Connection: SFC Edwin L. Clements was the Intel Sergeant on A-33 at Djirai (Jul 10, 1963 – Jan 11, 1964) [Sherman]. After Vietnam, Clements was Paul's team commander at Fort Bragg and issued Paul's Article 15 at Camp Lejeune on March 5, 1964 [A15]. The same NCO supervised Paul both in Vietnam and stateside.
  3. The Corder Connection: SP4 Henry D. "Andy" Corder Jr was the Radio Operator on A-33 at Djirai [Sherman]. Corder is the only A-33 member on SO 147 (M-14 rifle qualification, 26 May 1964) — a 34-name Co C order that also lists Paul [SO147]. No A-23 members appear on this order. A man Sherman placed at Djirai was qualifying alongside Paul at Bragg.
  4. The Rau Connection: 1LT Raymond R. Rau was the Det XO on A-33 at Djirai [Sherman]. The Article 15 document shows "Lt Rau" briefing Paul's unit at Bragg on 2 March 1964 as part of the 5th SFG S-1 [A15]. Three A-33 members (Clements, Rau, Corder) appear in Paul's chain of command at Bragg.
  5. Family stories match Djirai precisely: Mountains near Dalat, training Montagnards, heavy casualties on patrols, extended firefight ("two weeks, ran out of ammo and food"), CIA resupply drops [ORAL]. The ops reports for Djirai confirm all of this — Koho and Bulach tribesmen, two-company operations, 11 Strike Force KIA and 2 USSF WIA in November 1963 alone, three vehicle ambushes, VC Battalion E-300 operating in December [OPSUMS].
  6. Malaria → Nha Trang: Family account says Paul contracted malaria and was sent to Nha Trang (SF HQ) [ORAL]. Djirai is in the Central Highlands — the worst malaria zone in Vietnam. Nha Trang was the nearest SFOB with medical facilities. A malaria case from the Delta (Tan Phu) would more likely go to Saigon or Can Tho, not Nha Trang.
  7. Jump record supports highlands deployment: The August 30 mass tactical jump from an H-21 helicopter fits a highlands insertion [DA1307]. H-21s were the standard troop carrier for operations in the Central Highlands.
  8. SO 13 — Pre-deployment paperwork: Paul's MOS reclassification order (18 Jan 1963) lists him alongside 4 future A-33 Djirai teammates: Corder, Urquhart, Melton, and Shipley — all Co C, 5th SFG [SO13]. Six months before Vietnam, Paul was already being processed on the same orders as the men he'd serve with at Djirai.
  9. DA 66A — Independent Vietnam date confirmation: Five sequential DA Form 66A change reports (p.15-19) independently confirm the Vietnam dates as 25 Jul 63 – 17 Jan 64, matching the DD-214 exactly [DA66]. The pre-deployment physical (26 Jul 63) recorded Paul at 159 lbs; the post-return physical (20 Jan 64, three days after return) recorded 150 lbs — 9 pounds lost in 6 months of field duty in the Central Highlands.
  10. VA Stressor Letter (Paul's own claim narrative): Handwritten letter for a VA claim names camp "DA REI (DE REI s.i.c.) S.E. of Da Lat" — a strong phonetic/geographic match to Djirai in the highlands — and describes a mid-Nov '63 15-day hi-country combat operation with Montagnards, casualties, resupply, and being cut off. Names SP. 5 Meier (matches Joseph E. Meier Jr on the A-33 roster). Valuable first-person claim evidence; may contain memory errors (see full section below). Does not mention Tan Phu / Delta. [STRESSOR]

TAN PHU (A-23) — UNLIKELY (~20-25%)

  1. CIB Order SO 135: Paul is grouped with 9 confirmed A-23 Tan Phu members in the 5th SFG unit block [SO135]. The other A-33 members (Clements, Corder, Widder, etc.) do not appear on this order — their CIBs were on a different Special Order.
  2. Sherman's placement: Sherman listed Paul AND CPT Dobson on the A-23 page with notation "after 10/29/63?" [Sherman]. The Dobson pairing suggests Sherman had a source beyond SO 135 (Dobson as a medic/captain wouldn't be on the CIB order). However, the question mark indicates Sherman himself was uncertain. Sherman was asked directly what source led him to pair Barros and Dobson at A-23 — he did not respond, likely meaning he no longer recalls after 10+ years.
  3. A-23 demo specialist (per Sherman's May 2026 outreach) said no replacement arrived after the Oct 29 captures — a point against the replacement theory, but secondhand and not independently verified.
  4. Living A-23 contact (Sherman, Aug 2026): Sherman recently spoke with a living A-23 veteran. That man's recollection was not "Barros wasn't there" — his memory is now so poor that he cannot say one way or the other. This is not positive confirmation Paul was at A-23; it only weakens any earlier implication that an A-23 veteran's memory had ruled him out. Contact-status update only — does not change the documentary picture. [Sherman]
  5. Box 131 primary documents (Aug 2026): OCR of A-23 MOPSUMS (Jul–Dec 1963), the 8 Dec 1963 debrief, and Aug/Oct AARs found no "Barros" (or close variants). No named individual replacements for Versace/Rowe/Pitzer; CPT Dobson not found. See Box 131 section below. [BOX131]

The timeline and geography: Sherman's notation says "after 10/29/63?" — meaning as a replacement after the Rowe/Pitzer capture. Paul's 2nd combat jump (Oct 12) originated from Nha Trang — a highlands staging base, not Saigon or Can Tho (Delta) — consistent with returning to Djirai after malaria recovery. A Tan Phu assignment would require Paul to go from Djirai → Nha Trang (malaria) → back to Djirai (Oct 12 jump) → Tan Phu (after Oct 29) → depart Vietnam (Jan 17).

Note on C-3 augmentation practice: Sending augmentees between camps was standard C-3 procedure — CPT Roberts was sent from C-3 to A-22 after Srok Thom casualties, confirming the pattern. Paul being moved from one camp to another mid-tour would not have been unusual. The multiple moves alone are not an argument against Tan Phu.

Alternative explanations for the CIB grouping:

  • Paul's A-33 CIB recommendation was lost/delayed (Holland's emergency leave, Nov casualties disrupted admin) and resubmitted through a different chain, landing in a later batch with A-23 personnel.
  • C-3 HQ processed their augmentees' paperwork separately from the A-teams.
  • Batch processing at 5th SFG HQ combined unrelated recommendations on the same Special Order.

Why Tan Phu is unlikely: Paul told Djirai stories for 50 years — mountains, Montagnards, firefights, the extended patrol [ORAL]. He never once mentioned the Mekong Delta, canals, the U Minh Forest, or three Americans being captured. If he'd been sent to a camp where 3 men had just been captured by the VC — replacing one of them — that would be unforgettable. His complete silence across five decades is the strongest evidence against a Tan Phu assignment. Sherman's earlier report that an A-23 demo specialist recalled no replacement arriving is consistent with that lean, but a living A-23 contact's memory is now too poor to affirm or deny Paul's presence — so veteran recollection should not be read as having ruled him out.

Assessment: Djirai only ~65-70%. Djirai + brief Tan Phu ~20-25%. Other ~5-10%. Cannot be resolved without TDY orders from the 201 file.

Box 131 / A-23 primary-document review (August 2026) [BOX131]: NARA materials downloaded under folder label A1 1692; document slips identify Entry A1 1698 / Box 131. Reviewed: A-23 MOPSUMS Jul–Dec 1963, Dec 8 1963 detachment debrief, and Aug/Oct AARs.

  • No Barros: OCR found no "Barros" (or close variants) in these A-23 primary documents.
  • Confirms established A-23 picture: Capt Philip N. Arsenault CO; 1LT James N. Rowe XO; 29 Oct 1963 capture of Versace, Rowe, Pitzer; Delta terrain; team ~19 Jul 1963–Dec departure.
  • Replacements: Dec OPSUM refers to a whole-team "replacement detachment" / team departure from Vietnam — not named individual replacements for Versace/Rowe/Pitzer.
  • Dobson: not found in Box 131.
  • CA: Oct/Nov OPSUMs request a CA representative be replaced/sent ASAP (shortage of one team member; medics overloaded) — not a public-affairs label. Dec: Capt Hitchcock + CA rep civic-action plan.
  • Medic: Dec debrief medical sheet still lists SFC Pitzer (with Cooper, Van De Berg); no named USSF medic replacement for Pitzer. Dec "replacement" medic language = indigenous training by the replacement detachment.
  • Aug 3 1963 AAR (30 Jul defense): Friendly casualties = 3 Strike Force KIA, 10 Strike Force WIA, 1 USSF WIA — KIA unnamed; only named USSF casualty Len M. Corey (slightly wounded by mortar).
  • Oct 29 AAR: canal op / 306th VC Bn / three Americans captured — primary confirmation of material already in the research.

Implication for Paul: strengthens the A-23 historical picture and still does not place Paul at Tan Phu. Supports the Djirai-first assessment; Tan Phu remains an unresolved administrative puzzle (SO 135 / Arsenault block), not a documented assignment.

VA Stressor Letter — Paul's Own Claim Narrative [STRESSOR]

Source: Family photograph of the handwritten original titled "STRESSOR LETTER." VA File Number 28863192. This is Paul's own VA claim narrative — valuable oral/claim evidence of what he recalled for disability purposes. It may contain memory errors and should not silently overwrite settled documentary facts.

Full faithful transcript: docs/stressor-letter.txt (project); also /tmp/stressor_letter_barros.txt. Image copy: docs/stressor-letter-original.jpg.

Structured claims from the letter

  • Service: Oct. 22, 1961 – Oct. 23, 1964 (letter); unit claimed as Company "B", 5th SFG, 1st SF, Oct. 1962 – Oct. 1964
  • Arrival: June 1963 sent with a 10-man SF detachment to Na Trang (Nha Trang), South Vietnam
  • Camp: Nov. '63 — "A" Team at DA REI (DE REI s.i.c.), S.E. of Da Lat; fortified camp of 4 companies (~400) Montagnards
  • Named people: Major McGraw ("B" Co. Executive Officer); SP. 5 Meier; 2 unnamed SF NCOs; Paul
  • Mid-Nov '63 operation: SF element + 2 Montagnard companies into the hi-country to locate/neutralize a VC battalion HQ/complex; planned 3–7 days, lasted 15 days; 7-day march under daily mortar & automatic fire; medevac of Montagnard & ARVN wounded; out of food & ammo; Air Force B-24/B-26 support missed the target; after taking the objective, surrounded by VC regulars, held ~3 days under mortar/small arms, relieved by Montagnard & ARVN units
  • PTSD / stressor: Calls this the most traumatic & unforgettable of his patrols/operations; still has nightmares and flashbacks

Corroborations with the research record

  • "DA REI … S.E. of Da Lat" strongly supports Djirai (A-33) / Central Highlands — not Tan Phu / Mekong Delta. Aligns with family oral history and OPSUM geography.
  • SP. 5 Meier matches Joseph E. Meier Jr on Sherman's A-33 Djirai roster (demo, junior) — a named teammate in Paul's own hand.
  • 15-day combat / out of food & ammo / Montagnard companies / hi-country matches family oral history ("two weeks, ran out of ammo and food") and Nov '63 Djirai OPSUM intensity (two-company ops; heavy Strike Force casualties).
  • No Delta / Tan Phu / capture narrative — consistent with the Djirai-first lean and the long silence on Tan Phu.
  • Service end date Oct. 23, 1964 matches DD-214 separation day (start date off by one day vs documented enlistment Oct. 23, 1961).

Conflicts / memory-vs-record flags (do not silently overwrite)

  • Company "B" vs settled Company C: Letter claims Co "B" for Oct. 1962–Oct. 1964 and labels McGraw as "B" Co. XO. Multiple orders and DA forms place Paul in Company C, 5th SFG. Flag only — do not change the settled Co C assignment without documentary evidence. Possible memory conflation with a B-team label, C-3 usage, or later 6th SFG Co B separation processing (DA 66A Change #5 shows Co B, 6th SFG at separation).
  • June 1963 Na Trang vs Jul 25 TDY start: Letter remembers June arrival; DA 20 / DA 66A / Increment 6 (LO 6-872) document 25 July 1963 as Vietnam TDY start. Treat as memory compression around departure/staging vs formal in-country date.
  • Major McGraw: Not yet confirmed on the A-33 or C-3 rosters in this research; open lead.
  • Air Force B-24 / B-26: Period-plausible aircraft types for the era in theater; specific sortie not verified here against OPSUMs.
  • Spelling variants in the original (e.g., THREW, MOTAR, NETURIALIZE, DA REI/DE REI) are Paul's; Djirai identification rests on phonetics + "S.E. of Da Lat," not exact orthography.

Assessment impact: Adds a tenth, first-person line favoring Djirai; does not resolve the SO 135 / Arsenault-block Tan Phu puzzle and does not override Co C documentary assignment.
