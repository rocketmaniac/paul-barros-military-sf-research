STRESSOR LETTER

BARROS, PAUL N.
VA FILE NUMBER 28863192.

I SERVED WITH THE U.S. ARMY FROM OCT. 22, 1961 THREW
OCT. 23, 1964.
I SERVED WITH COMPANY "B", 5th SPECIAL FORCES GROUP, 1st
SPECIAL FORCES FROM OCT. 1962 TO OCT. 1964.
IN JUNE, 1963 I WAS SENT, WITH A 10 TEAM [DETACHMENT written above TEAM] OF SPECIAL FORCES
TO NA TRANG, SOUTH VIET NAM. IN NOV. '63 I WAS SENT TO AN
"A" TEAM STATIONED AT DA REI (DE REI s.i.c.) S.E. OF DA LAT.
THIS WAS A FORTIFIED CAMP OF 4 COMPANIES (APPROX. 400 MEN) OF
MONTAGARDS.
IN MID NOV. '63 MAJOR McGRAW ("B" CO. EX. OFFICER), SP. 5 MEIER,
2 S.F. NCO'S & MYSELF TOOK 2 COMPANIES OF MONTAGARS INTO
THE HI-COUNTRY TO LOCATE & NETURIALIZE A VIET CONG BATTILON
HQD/COMPLEX. IT WAS TO BE A 3 TO 7 DAY PATROL, INSTEAD IT
TURNED INTO A 15 DAY COMBAT OPERATION.
ON THE WAY TO THE OBJECTIVE WE WERE DAILY & CONSTANTLY SUBJECTED
TO MOTAR & AUTOMATIC FIRE FOR THE 7 DAYS OF THE MARCH.
WE CALLED IN MED-EVACK TO TAKE OUT THE CASUALITIES, MONTAGAR
& ARVN WOUNDED, AND RESUPPLY FOOD & AMMO. WE WERE OUT OF EVERYTHING.
WE ALSO CALLED IN AIR SUPPORT, AIR FORCE B-24 / B-26 BOMBER'S.
THEY MISSED THE TARGET.
AFTER REACHING AND TAKING THE OBJECTIVE WE WERE CUT OFF
AND SURROUND BY V.C. REGULAR UNITES & REPEATLY ATTACKED WITH
MOTAR & SMALL ARMS FIRE. WE WERE ABLE TO [HOLD written above] OUR POSITION UNTIL WE
WERE RELIVED 3 DAYS LATER BY MONTAGAR & ARVN UNITES.
I HAVE BEEN ON OTHER PATROWS & OPERATION'S BUT THIS WAS THE MOST
TRAUMATIC & UNFORGETTABLE. I STILL HAVE NIGHTMARES & FLASH BACKS
ABOUT THIS EXPERIENCE.

STRUCTURED CLAIMS (extracted; claim narrative — may contain memory errors)
============================================================

Veteran: Paul N. Barros
VA File Number: 28863192

Service dates (as claimed): Oct. 22, 1961 – Oct. 23, 1964
Unit (as claimed): Company "B", 5th Special Forces Group, 1st Special Forces
  (Oct. 1962 – Oct. 1964)
  NOTE: Documentary research places Paul in Company C, not B.

Vietnam arrival (as claimed): June 1963 to Na Trang (Nha Trang) with a
  10-man Special Forces detachment
  NOTE: Orders/forms show TDY start 25 Jul 1963 (Increment 6).

Camp (as claimed): Nov. '63 — "A" Team at DA REI (DE REI s.i.c.),
  S.E. of Da Lat; fortified camp of 4 companies (~400) Montagnards.
  Strong phonetic match to Djirai (A-33), Central Highlands.

Named personnel:
  - Major McGraw ("B" Co. Executive Officer)
  - SP. 5 Meier  [matches Joseph E. Meier Jr on A-33 Djirai roster]
  - 2 unnamed Special Forces NCOs
  - Paul himself

Mid-Nov 1963 operation:
  - Force: SF element + 2 companies Montagnards
  - Objective: locate & neutralize VC battalion HQ/complex in hi-country
  - Planned 3–7 days; lasted 15 days
  - 7 days march under daily mortar & automatic fire
  - Medevac of Montagnard & ARVN wounded; resupply food & ammo
  - Out of food and ammo
  - Air Force B-24/B-26 bombers called; missed target
  - After taking objective: surrounded by VC regulars; held 3 days
    under mortar/small arms; relieved by Montagnard & ARVN units

PTSD / stressor statement:
  Most traumatic & unforgettable experience among other patrols/ops;
  nightmares and flashbacks.

Notable original spellings: THREW, MOTAR, UNITES, NETURIALIZE,
BATTILON, MED-EVACK, CASUALITIES, MONTAGARDS/MONTAGAR(S),
RELIVED, REPEATLY, PATROWS, NA TRANG.
