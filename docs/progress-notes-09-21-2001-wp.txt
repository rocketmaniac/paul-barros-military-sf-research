21 SEP 2001 — MHC PSYCHOTHERAPY INDIVIDUAL (SOAP)
============================================================

NOTE DATED: 09/21/2001 11:33  MHC PSYCHOTHERAPY INDIVIDUAL
VISIT:      09/21/2001 10:30  C  MHCI/DEFIBAUGH

S - Vet stated he continues to work on reducing the amount of etoh
he consumed; that he has altogether quit drinking at home alone;
that he did have several drinks in a bar over the weekend. Vet
expressed motivation to begin attending VARC groups. I agreed to
network with VARC for him to enable this to happen. Vet expressed
he's having difficulty in writing about his RVN experiences. Vet
did describe a trauma in which he witnessed a white phosphorus
grenade go off on the torso of a fellow Green Beret. Vet given
#'s to various archives which may validate his trauma.

O - Vet was oriented x 3, had good hygiene, dressed appropriately,
was friendly and cooperative. Vet had a broad affect and
maintained eye contact. Vet is future oriented.

A - etoh abuse; r/o PTSD

P - Vet to continue keeping medical and mental health appts.;
adhere to his med regimen.

Signed by: /es/ CHARLES G DEFIBAUGH
CLINICAL SOCIAL WORKER  09/21/2001 11:41
Digital Pager: 216-388-1100
